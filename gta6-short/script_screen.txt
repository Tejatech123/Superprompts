Here's five crazy things you can do in GTA 6 that you never could in GTA 5.
Number one, pet the dog. For the first time in GTA, you actually can. In the gameplay reveal, Jason finds a dog in a hallway mid-mission. You get three choices: pet it, scold it, or study it. Pet it, and a little angel pops up. So even tiny choices now say who you are.
Number two, cops need a witness. If nobody sees it and no alarm goes off, it's never reported. But once they're onto you, they track your face, your clothes and your car. So you've got to change outfits, ditch the car, or split up. And the wanted level goes back up to six stars.
Number three, people fight back. Rockstar says it made over 600,000 animations for them. That's more than ten times GTA 5. Rob a store and some people run. Others reportedly walk right up and try to take your gun. So you can't treat a crowd like scenery anymore.
Number four, two leads, one couple. You can switch between Jason and Lucia most of the time. They text each other mid-mission as Jay and Lu. Rockstar says the relationship is yours to nurture or lose. No meter, just consequences. It's basically a crime romance.
Number five, Leonida is your playground. You can go scuba diving, rip an airboat through the swamps, and skydive straight over Vice City. Grab your binoculars, because there are 62 kinds of bird to find. No, really. Sixty-two.
