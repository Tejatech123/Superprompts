# Video 01: Why Does Apple Make More Profit Than Most Phone Brands Combined?

Length: about 75 s · about 200 words · 9:16 · ElevenLabs Eleven v3

## ElevenLabs VO (paste in 3 chunks)

**Chunk 1: Hook**
```
[curious] Apple sells only about one in every five smartphones in the world. [short pause] But it takes home most of the profit.
Samsung, Xiaomi, Vivo, Oppo... they sell more phones. So how does Apple earn more than almost all of them... combined?
Let's crack it in seventy seconds.
```

**Chunk 2: Body**
```
The obvious answer? iPhones are expensive. But a high price alone doesn't make you rich. Plenty of costly products fail.
Reason one... control. Apple designs its own chips and its own software. When you control both, you control the cost... and the price.
Reason two. Once you buy an iPhone... you rarely buy just one thing. AirPods connect in a second. The Watch works properly only with an iPhone. Your photos live in iCloud. Your chats live in iMessage.
Leaving means losing all of it. [short pause] This is called... the walled garden.
```

**Chunk 3: Reveal + close**
```
And reason three is where the real money is. Every app you buy, every subscription inside an app... Apple can take fifteen to thirty percent.
Add iCloud, Apple Music, Apple TV... and services bring in close to a hundred billion dollars a year, with margins far higher than the phones.
So Apple doesn't just sell you a phone. [short pause] It sells you a place to live... and collects rent every month.
Follow for one more "why", every day.
```

## Fact-check before posting
- "About one in five smartphones": Apple's global market share is roughly 18–20% by yearly shipments (IDC/Counterpoint). Check the latest year.
- "Most of the profit": Counterpoint has estimated Apple's share of global handset profit at around 80% in recent years. Check the latest figure.
- "15–30%": the App Store commission rates. Some regions (EU, US court rulings) now have exceptions, which the "can take" wording allows for.
- "Close to $100 billion": Apple's Services revenue was about $96B in FY2024. Check the latest annual report and update the line if it's now above $100B.
